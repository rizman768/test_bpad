# AGENTS.md

## AI Tool Usage

**Tool:** ChatGPT (OpenAI)

ChatGPT is used as a supporting tool during the development process, primarily for:

- Technical consultation and problem-solving.
- Understanding Laravel features and configurations.
- Discussing alternative implementation approaches.
- Assisting with debugging when encountering development issues.

AI assistance is limited to providing suggestions, explanations, and technical references. Application development, code implementation, testing, and final decisions are carried out by the developer.

## Responsibility

The developer remains responsible for the entire development process, including implementation, validation, testing, and maintenance of the application.
